Episode 2: 今日は あめですか。

Is it rainy today? · 2026-11-19 · Topic: the weather today

Today's pattern

AはBですか · A wa B desu ka
= Is A B?
  • A: the topic: a thing, a person, a time word like today or tomorrow, or 'this' (これ)
  • B: a noun you're asking about: weather (あめ, はれ, くもり), a drink (コーヒー, おちゃ), a person's name or job

Building practical sentences

step 1 今日は kyou wa → As for today
step 2 今日はあめです。 kyou wa ame desu. → Today is rainy.
step 3 今日はあめですか。 kyou wa ame desu ka. → Is it rainy today?
step 1 これは kore wa → As for this
step 2 これはコーヒーです。 kore wa koohii desu. → This is coffee.
step 3 これはコーヒーですか。 kore wa koohii desu ka. → Is this coffee?
step 1 あしたは ashita wa → As for tomorrow
step 2 あしたははれです。 ashita wa hare desu. → Tomorrow will be sunny.
step 3 あしたははれですか。 ashita wa hare desu ka. → Will it be sunny tomorrow?

💡 Tip: か is a question mark you can hear. You don't change the word order or add a separate word for 'is'. Just take any AはBです sentence and put か at the end. To answer, start with はい (yes) or いいえ (no).

Real Japanese (radio host asking the weather forecaster)

では、しゅうまつは はれですか。
So then, will the weekend be sunny?

〜ですか — Yes/no questions with か

[Topic] は [Noun] ですか。

To turn a statement into a yes/no question in Japanese, you don't change the word order at all. You just add the small particle か (ka) to the very end. So 今日は あめです (Today is rain) becomes 今日は あめですか (Is today rain? = Is it rainy today?). か works like a spoken question mark, so a full Japanese question still ends with 。 and not ？. When you answer, you can start with はい (yes) or いいえ (no) and often drop the topic, as in いいえ、くもりです (No, it's cloudy).

  • Slot order: [Topic] は + [Noun] + です + か. The new part is only the last slot, か.
  • Unlike English, nothing moves. English swaps 'it is' to 'is it', but Japanese keeps the order and just adds か at the end.
  • か is a particle that marks the whole sentence as a question. Your voice usually rises a little on か.
  • In answers, the topic is often left out because everyone already knows it: (今日は) くもりです → くもりです.
  • Weather words like あめ (rain), はれ (sunny) and くもり (cloudy) are nouns here, so they fit straight into the [Noun] slot before です.

This is the X は Y です pattern from episode 1 with one extra particle, か, added at the end. Any statement you can already make, you can now ask as a question.

Teacher script

show

Welcome back! Today's pattern is AはBですか. It means: Is A B? It's how you ask a yes-or-no question.

🔊 AはBですか。

Here's the good news: you already know almost all of it. Last time you learned AはBです, which means A is B. Today we add just one tiny sound at the very end: か.

Let's walk through the slots. A is the topic, the thing you're talking about. Then comes は, the little word that marks the topic. B is the noun you're asking about. Then です, which means 'is'. And last, か, which turns everything into a question.

Notice the word order. English moves 'is' to the front to make a question: 'It is rain' becomes 'Is it rain?' Japanese doesn't move anything. The order stays the same, and the question word か just goes at the end.

Let's build one. Start with the topic, 今日, which means today, plus は.

🔊 今日は

As for today. Now add あめ, rain, and です.

🔊 今日はあめです。

Today is rainy. That's a statement. Now add か.

🔊 今日はあめですか。

Is it rainy today? That's exactly what Ken asks in today's dialogue.

Another one. Start with これは, which means 'as for this'.

🔊 これは

Add コーヒー, coffee, and です.

🔊 これはコーヒーです。

This is coffee. Now finish it with か.

🔊 これはコーヒーですか。

Is this coffee? And to answer, say はい, yes, or いいえ, no, then the real answer.

🔊 いいえ、おちゃです。

No, it's tea.

Now a swap. Keep the same skeleton, but use あした, tomorrow, and はれ, sunny weather.

🔊 あしたははれですか。

Will it be sunny tomorrow? Same frame, new words. Put any topic in A and any noun in B.

Here's a memory trick: think of か as a question mark you can hear. Beginners often think they need a rising voice or a different word order. You don't. か does the work all by itself.

So here's how it snaps onto what you know: any AはBです sentence becomes a question when you add か. Listen to the pair one more time.

🔊 おちゃです。おちゃですか。

It's tea. Is it tea? Now let's hear Yuki and Ken use it.

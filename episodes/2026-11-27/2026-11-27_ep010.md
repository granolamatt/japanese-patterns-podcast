## Today's pattern

### AはBです／AはBじゃないです  ·  *A wa B desu / A wa B ja nai desu*  =  **A is B / A isn't B (B = a na-adjective)**

- **A**: the topic: a person, thing or place (おかあさん, 私のかぞく, このへや), often built with の or この
- **B**: a な-adjective describing A: きれい (pretty / clean), げんき (energetic, well), しずか (quiet), ゆうめい (famous), ふあんてい (unstable). Used plainly, with no な before です

**な-adjective + です** — な-adjectives (and their negative じゃないです)

Structure: `[Topic] は [な-adj] です／じゃないです。`

### Building practical sentences

1. おかあさん  *okaasan*  → mother
2. おかあさんは  *okaasan wa*  → as for mother
3. おかあさんはげんき  *okaasan wa genki*  → mother… energetic
4. おかあさんはげんきです。  *Okaasan wa genki desu.*  → My mother is full of energy.

1. 私のかぞくは  *watashi no kazoku wa*  → as for my family
2. 私のかぞくはしずか  *watashi no kazoku wa shizuka*  → my family… quiet
3. 私のかぞくはしずかじゃないです。  *Watashi no kazoku wa shizuka ja nai desu.*  → My family isn't quiet.

1. このへやは  *kono heya wa*  → as for this room
2. このへやはきれいです。  *Kono heya wa kirei desu.*  → This room is clean.
3. このへやはきれいじゃないです。  *Kono heya wa kirei ja nai desu.*  → This room isn't clean.

> **Tip:** きれい ends in an い sound, but it's a な-adjective, so its negative is きれいじゃないです, never きれいくないです. A memory trick: な-adjectives act like nouns (add です or じゃないです), and い-adjectives change their own ending (〜くないです).

### Real Japanese (TV weather forecast)

**明日の天気は不安定です。**  
Tomorrow's weather will be unstable.

な-adjectives are a second kind of describing word. As a predicate (the part at the end of the sentence) they fit the same slot as a noun: [Topic] は [な-adj] です. To make it negative, swap です for じゃないです, the same as with nouns: おとうさんは しずかじゃないです. This is the big difference from い-adjectives. With those you change the word itself (たかい → たかくない). With な-adjectives the word never changes. Only the ending after it changes.

- Slot order: [Topic] + は + [な-adj] + です. The adjective always comes right before です, at the end of the sentence.
- Negative: [Topic] は [な-adj] じゃないです. Nothing is added to the adjective itself. You only swap です for じゃないです.
- Compare い-adjectives: おいしい → おいしくないです (the word changes). な-adjectives: しずか → しずかじゃないです (the word stays the same).
- Questions work as before: add か at the end. おとうさんも げんきですか。 (Is your father well too?)
- Watch out: きれい ends in い but is a な-adjective. The negative is きれいじゃないです, never きれくないです.

*な-adjectives work exactly like nouns in 〜は〜です and 〜じゃないです (かぞくは しずかじゃないです has the same shape as 私は 学生じゃないです). They are unlike い-adjectives, which change form to 〜くないです.*

### Swap-in examples

1. おとうさんはげんきです。  
   *Otousan wa genki desu.*  
   My father is doing well.  (swap → topic: おかあさん → おとうさん)

2. このみせはしずかです。  
   *Kono mise wa shizuka desu.*  
   This shop is quiet.  (swap → topic: おとうさん → このみせ; adjective: げんき → しずか)

3. このみせはしずかじゃないです。  
   *Kono mise wa shizuka janaidesu.*  
   This shop isn't quiet.  (swap → ending: です → じゃないです)

4. えきのトイレはきれいですか。  
   *Eki no toire wa kirei desu ka.*  
   Is the station toilet clean?  (swap → topic: このみせ → えきのトイレ; adjective: しずか → きれい; made into a question with か)

5. ゆきのかぞくもげんきです。  
   *Yuki no kazoku mo genki desu.*  
   Yuki's family is doing well too.  (swap → topic: えきのトイレ → ゆきのかぞく; particle: は → も)

## Dialogue

**1. Yuki:** けん、あれはだれですか。  
*Ken, are wa dare desu ka.*  
Ken, who is that over there?  
Literal: `Ken, that-over-there TOPIC who is QUESTION`

| けん | あれ | は | だれ | です | か |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ken | are | wa | dare | desu | ka |
| *Ken* | *that (over there)* | *topic marker* | *who* | *is* | *question marker* |

**2. Ken:** あれは私(わたし)のおかあさんです。  
*Are wa watashi no okaasan desu.*  
That's my mother.  
Literal: `that-over-there TOPIC I 'S mother is`

| あれ | は | 私(わたし) | の | おかあさん | です |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| are | wa | watashi | no | okaasan | desu |
| *that (over there)* | *topic marker* | *I* | *'s (links nouns)* | *mother* | *is* |

**3. Yuki:** そうですか。おかあさんはきれいです。  
*Soudesuka. Okaasan wa kirei desu.*  
Oh, really? Your mother is pretty.  
Literal: `oh-really. mother TOPIC pretty is`

| そうですか | おかあさん | は | きれい | です |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| soudesuka | okaasan | wa | kirei | desu |
| *oh, really?* | *mother* | *topic marker* | *pretty* | *is* |

**4. Ken:** ありがとうございます。おかあさんはげんきです。  
*Arigatougozaimasu. Okaasan wa genki desu.*  
Thank you. My mother is full of energy.  
Literal: `thank-you. mother TOPIC energetic is`

| ありがとうございます | おかあさん | は | げんき | です |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| arigatougozaimasu | okaasan | wa | genki | desu |
| *thank you* | *mother* | *topic marker* | *healthy, energetic* | *is* |

**5. Yuki:** おとうさんもげんきですか。  
*Otousan mo genki desu ka.*  
Is your father doing well too?  
Literal: `father ALSO energetic is QUESTION`

| おとうさん | も | げんき | です | か |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| otousan | mo | genki | desu | ka |
| *father* | *also (replaces は)* | *healthy, energetic* | *is* | *question marker* |

**6. Ken:** ええ、げんきです。おとうさんはしずかです。  
*Ee, genki desu. Otousan wa shizuka desu.*  
Yeah, he's well. My father is quiet.  
Literal: `yeah, energetic is. father TOPIC quiet is`

| ええ | げんき | です | おとうさん | は | しずか | です |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ee | genki | desu | otousan | wa | shizuka | desu |
| *yeah* | *healthy, energetic* | *is* | *father* | *topic marker* | *quiet* | *is* |

**7. Yuki:** 私(わたし)のかぞくはしずかじゃないです。  
*Watashi no kazoku wa shizuka janaidesu.*  
My family isn't quiet.  
Literal: `I 'S family TOPIC quiet is-not`

| 私(わたし) | の | かぞく | は | しずか | じゃないです |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| watashi | no | kazoku | wa | shizuka | janaidesu |
| *I* | *'s (links nouns)* | *family* | *topic marker* | *quiet* | *is not* |

**8. Ken:** そうですか。これはおかあさんのおにぎりです。どうぞ。  
*Soudesuka. Kore wa okaasan no onigiri desu. Douzo.*  
Oh, really? This is my mother's rice ball. Here you go.  
Literal: `oh-really. this TOPIC mother 'S rice-ball is. here-you-go`

| そうですか | これ | は | おかあさん | の | おにぎり | です | どうぞ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| soudesuka | kore | wa | okaasan | no | onigiri | desu | douzo |
| *oh, really?* | *this* | *topic marker* | *mother* | *'s (links nouns)* | *rice ball* | *is* | *here you go* |

**9. Yuki:** いただきます。つめたくないです。おいしいです！  
*Itadakimasu. Tsumetakunai desu. Oishii desu!*  
Itadakimasu. It's not cold. It's delicious!  
Literal: `itadakimasu. not-cold is. delicious is!`

| いただきます | つめたくない | です | おいしい | です |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| itadakimasu | tsumetakunai | desu | oishii | desu |
| *(said before eating)* | *not cold* | *is (polite)* | *delicious* | *is (polite)* |

**10. Ken:** そうですね。コンビニのおにぎりじゃないです。  
*Soudesune. Konbini no onigiri janaidesu.*  
That's right. It's not a convenience-store rice ball.  
Literal: `that's-right. convenience-store 'S rice-ball is-not`

| そうですね | コンビニ | の | おにぎり | じゃないです |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| soudesune | konbini | no | onigiri | janaidesu |
| *that's right* | *convenience store* | *'s / from (links nouns)* | *rice ball* | *is not* |

## Teacher script

Welcome back! Today's pattern lets you say what something is like, using a new family of describing words. Here it is.  
> 🔊 AはBです  
It means: A is B. Here, B is a describing word called a **な**-adjective. The negative is this:  
> 🔊 AはBじゃないです  
A is not B. So: A is pretty, or A isn't quiet.  
Let's go through the slots. A is the topic, the person or thing you're talking about. Next comes **は**, the topic marker you already know.  
Slot B is the **な**-adjective. Three of them come up in today's dialogue: **きれい**, which means pretty or clean, **げんき**, which means energetic or well, and **しずか**, which means quiet.  
Then **です** comes last, just like with nouns. The word order is topic, then description, then **です**. In English, the word is comes in the middle. In Japanese, it comes at the very end.  
Let's build one. Start with the topic: mother.  
> 🔊 おかあさん  
Add the topic marker.  
> 🔊 おかあさんは  
As for mother. Now add the description, energetic.  
> 🔊 おかあさんはげんき  
Finish with **です**.  
> 🔊 おかあさんはげんきです。  
My mother is full of energy.  
Now a negative one. Start with my family. Remember that **の** links nouns.  
> 🔊 私のかぞくは  
As for my family. Add quiet.  
> 🔊 私のかぞくはしずか  
Now end with **じゃないです** instead of **です**.  
> 🔊 私のかぞくはしずかじゃないです。  
My family isn't quiet.  
Here's a swap. Keep the skeleton and change the words. The topic becomes this room, and we bring back **きれい**, this time meaning clean.  
> 🔊 このへやはきれいです。  
This room is clean. And the negative:  
> 🔊 このへやはきれいじゃないです。  
This room isn't clean. The frame stays the same and only the pieces change.  
Now a common trap. The word **きれい** ends in an **い** sound, so learners treat it like an **い**-adjective and say this, which is wrong:  
> 🔊 きれいくないです  
That's a mistake. **きれい** is a **な**-adjective, so the negative is **きれいじゃないです**. Here's a memory trick: **な**-adjectives act like nouns, and **い**-adjectives change their own ending.  
That's also how today's pattern snaps onto what you know. Since **な**-adjectives act like nouns, **じゃないです** is the same negative you learned for nouns. Add **か** to ask a question. Swap **は** for **も** to say also. In the dialogue, Yuki puts all of that together:  
> 🔊 おとうさんもげんきですか。  
Is your father doing well too? Now let's hear Yuki and Ken use it.

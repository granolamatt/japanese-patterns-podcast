Episode 8: この シャツは かわいいです

This Shirt Is Cute! (Shopping and Prices) · 2026-11-25 · Topic: shopping for clothes and asking prices

Today's pattern

AはBです（B＝い形容詞） · A wa B desu (B = an i-adjective)
= A is B (B = a describing word like cute, expensive, cheap)
  • A: The topic: a thing, person or place, e.g. これ, それ, このシャツ
  • B: An い-adjective, a describing word that ends in い: かわいい (cute), たかい (expensive), やすい (cheap), さむい (cold)

💡 Tip: The final い is part of an い-adjective, so never drop it: たかいです, not たかです. Think of い as the adjective's little tail that always stays on. Also remember word order: the describing word comes first and です always comes last.

Real Japanese (TV weather report)

あしたのとうきょうは、さむいです。
Tomorrow, Tokyo will be cold. (Literally: As for tomorrow's Tokyo, it is cold.)

い-adjective + です — い-adjectives as the predicate

[Topic] は [い-adj] です。

Until now, the slot before です always held a noun: これは シャツです (This is a shirt). Today a describing word goes into that same slot: この シャツは かわいいです (This shirt is cute). These describing words are called い-adjectives because they all end in い: かわいい (cute), たかい (expensive), やすい (cheap). The frame stays the same: topic + は, then the adjective, then です. Here です mostly makes the sentence polite. The adjective already carries the meaning 'is cute'.

  • Word order: [Topic] は [い-adjective] です。 → この シャツは たかいです。 = 'this shirt TOPIC expensive is'. The adjective comes at the end, just before です, the same as a noun did.
  • は marks what you are talking about (the shirt). The adjective after it tells you what the topic is like.
  • The final い is part of the adjective, so keep it. Say たかいです, not たかです.
  • To ask a question, add か as usual: それは たかいですか。 = Is that expensive?
  • Watch out: to say 'not expensive', you can't just use じゃないです with い-adjectives. That is a different form, and we'll learn it in a later episode. For now, only use the positive form.

This is the 〜は〜です frame from episode 1 with an adjective in the noun's place. も still swaps in for は (それも かわいいです = that one is cute too), and この/それ still pick out which thing the topic is.

Teacher script

show

Welcome back! Today's pattern is AはBです, where B is a describing word. It means: A is B. For example, this shirt is cute.

You already know this skeleton from nouns. Today, the B slot holds an adjective instead of a noun.

Slot A is the topic, the thing you're talking about. The little word は marks it, like saying, 'as for this.'

Slot B is the describing word. Today's describing words all end in the sound い. Here are three: かわいい, cute. たかい, expensive. やすい, cheap.

And です comes last, as always. It makes the sentence polite and finished. In English, 'is' goes in the middle. In Japanese, the describing word comes first, then です at the very end.

Let's build one. Start with the topic: this shirt.

🔊 このシャツ

That's 'this shirt.' Now add the topic marker.

🔊 このシャツは

'As for this shirt...' Now add 'cute' and finish with です.

🔊 このシャツはかわいいです。

This shirt is cute.

Here's another one, about a price. Start with 'that.'

🔊 それ

🔊 それは

'As for that...' Now add 'expensive' and です.

🔊 それはたかいです。

That's expensive.

One more. Start with 'this,' add は, then 'cheap.'

🔊 これ

🔊 これは

🔊 これはやすいです。

This is cheap.

Now a swap. Keep 'this shirt,' and just change the describing word.

🔊 このシャツはたかいです。

This shirt is expensive.

🔊 このシャツはやすいです。

This shirt is cheap. Same skeleton, new word. That's the whole trick.

A common mistake: cutting off the last sound. The い is part of the word, so always keep it. Say たかいです, never stop early. Think of い as the adjective's little tail, and it always stays on.

Your old patterns work here too. Add か to ask a question.

🔊 それはたかいですか。

Is that expensive? And swap は for も to say 'too.'

🔊 それもかわいいです。

That one is cute too. Listen for the describing words and the です at the end. Now let's hear Yuki and Ken use it.

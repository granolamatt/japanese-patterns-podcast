# Episode 5: これは あさごはんです — This Is My Breakfast
*2026-11-22 · Topic: morning routine · Audio: `2026-11-22_ep005.mp3`*

## Today's pattern

### これはAです／このAはBです  ·  *kore wa A desu / kono A wa B desu*  =  **This is A / This A is B**

- **これ／それ／あれ**: これ = this (near me), それ = that (near you), あれ = that over there (far from both of us). They stand alone as the topic, with no noun after them.
- **この／その／あの**: The same three distances, but they must come right before a noun: このパン (this bread), そのかさ (that umbrella), あのケーキ (that cake over there).
- **A**: A thing: food, a drink, an object (おちゃ, パン, かさ, ケーキ)
- **B**: A description noun, often with の: みせのパン, 私のかさ, ともだちのケーキ

**これ／それ／あれ・この〜** — This / that / that over there

Structure: `これ は [Noun] です。 / この [Noun] は …`

### Building practical sentences

1. パン  *pan*  → bread
2. このパン  *kono pan*  → this bread
3. このパンは  *kono pan wa*  → as for this bread
4. このパンはみせのパンです。  *kono pan wa mise no pan desu.*  → This bread is from the shop.

1. あれは  *are wa*  → as for that over there
2. あれはケーキです。  *are wa keeki desu.*  → That over there is cake.
3. あれはケーキですか。  *are wa keeki desu ka.*  → Is that over there cake?

1. このかさは  *kono kasa wa*  → as for this umbrella
2. このかさはけんのかさですか。  *kono kasa wa Ken no kasa desu ka.*  → Is this umbrella Ken's?
3. はい、それは私のかさです。  *hai, sore wa watashi no kasa desu.*  → Yes, that's my umbrella.

> **Tip:** これ／それ／あれ stand alone, but この／その／あの always need a noun right after them. Never say これパン. Say このパン, or これはパンです. Memory trick: the の in この is like the linker の, and it always needs a noun after it.

### Real Japanese (store announcement)

**このケーキは、本日のおすすめです。**  
This cake is today's recommendation.

これ, それ and あれ are words that point at things, and they fill the topic slot just like any other noun: これ は [Noun] です = 'This is [Noun].' Which one you use depends on distance: これ is near the speaker, それ is near the listener, and あれ is far from both of you. この, その and あの work differently. They can't stand alone. They must sit right before a noun, the way 'this' works in 'this bread': この パン は … = 'This bread is …'. That whole chunk, この + noun, then takes the topic marker は.

- これ は おちゃ です。 = [pointing word] + は (topic marker) + [noun] + です. It's the same frame as 私 は 学生 です, just with これ in the topic slot.
- この always comes right before a noun: この パン (this bread), この かさ (this umbrella). You can't say この は, but これ は is fine.
- Distance: これ/この = near me, それ/その = near you, あれ/あの = over there. When Yuki asks about 'this umbrella' (この かさ) in her hand, Ken answers with それ ('that one, near you').
- You can still add か for a question or use じゃないです for 'is not': あれ は ケーキ です か。 / コーヒー じゃないです。
- の still links nouns inside the sentence: この パン は みせ の パン です。 = this bread TOPIC shop 'S bread is.

*This is the same [Topic] は [Noun] です frame from episode 1. Now これ/それ/あれ go in the topic slot, and この goes right before a noun to make the topic. It works with か questions, じゃないです negatives and の phrases like 私の.*

### Swap-in examples

1. これは私(わたし)のコーヒーです。  
   *Kore wa watashi no koohii desu.*  
   This is my coffee.  (swap → base sentence)

2. それは私(わたし)のコーヒーです。  
   *Sore wa watashi no koohii desu.*  
   That (near you) is my coffee.  (swap → pointing word: これ → それ)

3. あれはともだちのコーヒーです。  
   *Are wa tomodachi no koohii desu.*  
   That over there is my friend's coffee.  (swap → pointing word: それ → あれ; owner: 私 → ともだち)

4. このコーヒーはみせのコーヒーです。  
   *Kono koohii wa mise no koohii desu.*  
   This coffee is from the shop.  (swap → topic: これ → この コーヒー; owner: みせ)

5. このケーキはみせのケーキですか。  
   *Kono keeki wa mise no keeki desu ka.*  
   Is this cake from the shop?  (swap → noun: コーヒー → ケーキ (+ か question))

## Teacher script

And its partner is **このAはBです**. It means: This A is B. Let's take them apart.  
Japanese has three pointing words, based on distance. **これ** means this, something near me.  
**それ** means that, something near you, the listener. And **あれ** means that over there, far from both of us.  
These three stand alone. Put **は** after them, and you have your topic, just like last time. Then the noun, then **です** last.  
> 🔊 これはおちゃです。  
This is tea. Word by word: this, topic marker, tea, is. The **です** comes at the very end.  
Now the second set. Swap the ending sound and you get **この**, **その**, **あの**. These cannot stand alone. They must hold hands with a noun right after them.  
Let's build one. Start with the thing.  
> 🔊 パン  
Bread. Now point at it.  
> 🔊 このパン  
This bread. Add the topic marker.  
> 🔊 このパンは

> 🔊 このパンはみせのパンです。  
This bread is from the shop. Now let's build a question. Start with the far-away word.  
> 🔊 あれは  
As for that over there. Add the noun and **です**.  
> 🔊 あれはケーキです。  
That over there is cake. Add **か** at the end, and it's a question.  
> 🔊 あれはケーキですか。  
Is that over there cake? One more. Start with umbrella, and point at it.  
> 🔊 このかさは

> 🔊 それはコーヒーじゃないです。  
That's not coffee. Pointing word, **は**, noun, ending. Same frame as always.

Episode 3: 学生じゃないです

I'm Not a Student · 2026-11-20 · Topic: self-introductions

Today's pattern

AはBじゃないです · A wa B ja nai desu
= A is not B
  • A: the topic: a person (私, a name like けん), a thing (これ), or a time or place (あした, きょう)
  • B: a noun that A is NOT: a job (学生, かいしゃいん, せんせい), a nationality (日本人), a thing (コーヒー), or the weather (雨)

Building practical sentences

step 1 私は watashi wa → As for me...
step 2 私は学生 watashi wa gakusei → As for me... student
step 3 私は学生じゃないです。 watashi wa gakusei ja nai desu. → I'm not a student.
step 1 これは kore wa → As for this...
step 2 これはコーヒー kore wa koohii → As for this... coffee
step 3 これはコーヒーじゃないです。 kore wa koohii ja nai desu. → This isn't coffee.
step 1 私は watashi wa → As for me...
step 2 私は日本人 watashi wa nihonjin → As for me... Japanese person
step 3 私は日本人じゃないです。 watashi wa nihonjin ja nai desu. → I'm not Japanese.

💡 Tip: The negative ending じゃないです replaces です. It doesn't come after it, so never say "desu ja nai desu." And because "not" comes at the very end in Japanese, always listen to the last word to know whether a sentence is yes or no.

Real Japanese (TV weather report)

あしたは雨じゃないです。
Tomorrow, it won't be rain.

〜じゃないです — X is not Y (noun negative)

[Topic] は [Noun] じゃないです。

To say that X is NOT Y, take the sentence you already know, [Topic] は [Noun] です, and replace です with じゃないです. Nothing else moves: the topic still comes first and is marked with は, the noun still sits in the middle, and the ending still comes last. So 私は 学生です (I am a student) becomes 私は 学生じゃないです (I am not a student). In Japanese, whether a sentence is positive or negative is only decided at the very end, so you have to listen all the way to the last word.

  • Slot order: [Topic] + は + [Noun] + じゃないです。 The 'is not' part always comes last, after the noun.
  • は marks the topic, meaning what we are talking about. It does not change in a negative sentence.
  • じゃないです works as one block that means 'is not / am not / are not'. It attaches straight to the noun, with no particle in between.
  • A common way to answer: say 'no' with いいえ, use じゃないです for the wrong idea, then give the right one with です. Example: いいえ、私は 学生じゃないです。かいしゃいんです。
  • In the second sentence (かいしゃいんです), the topic 私は is left out because everyone already knows who we mean.

This is the negative version of 〜は〜です from episode 1. It is also the natural 'no' answer to the 〜ですか questions from episode 2: if someone asks 学生ですか, you can reply いいえ、学生じゃないです.

Teacher script

show

Welcome back! Today's pattern is AはBじゃないです. It means: A is not B.

🔊 AはBじゃないです。

That's A is not B. You already know AはBです, which means A is B. Today we just make it negative.

Here are the slots. A is the topic: a person, a thing, whatever you're talking about. The little word は comes right after it and marks it as the topic.

B is a noun, like a job, a nationality, or a drink. Then, where you used to say です, you now say じゃないです. That ending means is not.

Notice the word order. In English, the word not comes in the middle: I am not a student. In Japanese, the not comes at the very end, glued to the ending. You don't know it's negative until the last moment, so listen to the end of the sentence!

Let's build one. Start with I.

🔊 私

Add the topic marker.

🔊 私は

As for me. Now add the noun 学生, student.

🔊 私は学生

And finish with the negative ending.

🔊 私は学生じゃないです。

I'm not a student. That's exactly what Ken says in today's dialogue.

Another one. Start with これ, this.

🔊 これは

As for this. Add コーヒー, coffee.

🔊 これはコーヒー

Now close it with is not.

🔊 これはコーヒーじゃないです。

This isn't coffee. Now a swap: same skeleton, new noun. Put 日本人, Japanese person, in the B slot.

🔊 私は日本人じゃないです。

I'm not Japanese. Same frame, new word. You can drop in any noun you know.

A common mistake: saying です and then trying to add the negative after it. The negative replaces です with じゃないです. There's only one です, and it's at the very end.

Now let's snap it onto what you know. Someone asks a question with か:

🔊 けんは学生ですか。

Ken, are you a student? You answer with いいえ, no, then today's pattern, then the true fact with plain です.

🔊 いいえ、私は学生じゃないです。かいしゃいんです。

No, I'm not a student. I'm an office worker. Question, no, is not, is. That's a full conversation!

Now let's hear Yuki and Ken use it.

# Episode 11: たのしい しゅみ — A Fun Hobby
*2026-11-28 · Topic: hobbies · Audio: `2026-11-28_ep011.mp3`*

## Today's pattern

### A N ／ B な N  ·  *A N / B na N*  =  **an A N / a B N (the adjective comes before the noun, just like in English)**

- **A**: an い-adjective in its dictionary form, attached directly to the noun: おいしい, あつい, つめたい, たのしい, おおきい
- **B**: a な-adjective followed by な: しずか, きれい, ゆうめい, げんき
- **N**: any noun: コーヒー, ケーキ, みせ, やすみ, まち, よてい

**adjective + noun** — Adjectives in front of nouns (おいしい コーヒー / しずかな まち)

Structure: `[い-adj] [Noun] / [な-adj] な [Noun]`

### Building practical sentences

1. コーヒー  *koohii*  → coffee
2. あつい コーヒー  *atsui koohii*  → hot coffee
3. あつい コーヒーです。  *atsui koohii desu.*  → It's hot coffee.
4. これは あつい コーヒーです。  *kore wa atsui koohii desu.*  → This is hot coffee.

1. みせ  *mise*  → shop
2. しずかな みせ  *shizuka na mise*  → a quiet shop
3. あれは しずかな みせです。  *are wa shizuka na mise desu.*  → That over there is a quiet shop.

> **Tip:** Never put な after an い-adjective: it's あつい コーヒー, not あついな コーヒー. Memory trick: い-adjectives stand on their own; な-adjectives need な to hold the noun's hand. And な only appears in front of a noun. Before です, it's just しずかです.

### Real Japanese (morning weather report)

**きょうの とうきょうは さむい あさです。**  
Tokyo is having a cold morning today.

Until now, adjectives always came at the end of the sentence: ケーキは おいしいです (The cake is delicious). This time the adjective moves to the front of a noun and describes it directly: おいしい ケーキ (delicious cake). The adjective and the noun now work as one block, and that block can go anywhere a plain noun can go: before です, before は or も, or after の. い-adjectives go straight in front of the noun with no change. な-adjectives need a な between the adjective and the noun: しずかな みせ (a quiet shop).

- Japanese puts the describing word first, like English: [adjective] [noun]. おいしい コーヒー = delicious coffee, たのしい よてい = fun plan.
- い-adjectives attach directly: つめたい ケーキ, あつい コーヒー. Don't add anything in between.
- な-adjectives need な before the noun: しずか → しずかな みせ, きれい → きれいな かさ. Leaving out the な is a common beginner mistake.
- The [adjective + noun] block is still just a noun phrase. It takes the same slots a noun does: 'たのしい よてい です', 'あつい コーヒー も どうぞ', 'しずかな みせ の コーヒー'.
- Compare the two positions. 'ケーキ は おいしい です' is a statement about the cake (the adjective comes at the end). 'おいしい ケーキ です' just names the thing (the adjective sits inside the noun phrase).

*In episodes 8 to 10 you used い- and な-adjectives at the end of a sentence with です. In this episode the same adjectives move in front of a noun, and な-adjectives get a な on the end when they do. The noun phrase they form fits into patterns you already know, like 〜は〜です, 〜も and 〜の〜.*

### Swap-in examples

1. これはおいしいコーヒーです。  
   *Kore wa oishii koohii desu.*  
   This is good coffee.  (swap → base sentence)

2. これはおいしいおちゃです。  
   *Kore wa oishii ocha desu.*  
   This is good tea.  (swap → noun: コーヒー → おちゃ)

3. これはつめたいおちゃです。  
   *Kore wa tsumetai ocha desu.*  
   This is cold tea.  (swap → い-adjective: おいしい → つめたい)

4. これはきれいなかさです。  
   *Kore wa kireina kasa desu.*  
   This is a pretty umbrella.  (swap → い-adj + noun → な-adj + な + noun: つめたい おちゃ → きれいな かさ)

5. あれはしずかなみせじゃないです。  
   *Are wa shizukana mise janaidesu.*  
   That place over there isn't a quiet shop.  (swap → topic and ending: これ → あれ, です → じゃないです (with しずかな みせ))

## Dialogue

**1. Yuki:** けんのしゅみは何(なん)ですか。  
*Ken no shumi wa nan desu ka.*  
Ken, what's your hobby?  
Literal: `Ken 'S hobby TOPIC what is QUESTION`

| けん | の | しゅみ | は | 何(なん) | です | か |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ken | no | shumi | wa | nan | desu | ka |
| *Ken* | *'s (links nouns)* | *hobby* | *topic marker* | *what* | *is* | *question marker* |

**2. Ken:** えいがです。にちようびのえいがはたのしいやすみです。  
*Eiga desu. Nichiyoubi no eiga wa tanoshii yasumi desu.*  
Movies. A Sunday movie makes a fun day off.  
Literal: `movie is. Sunday 'S movie TOPIC fun day-off is`

| えいが | です | にちようび | の | えいが | は | たのしい | やすみ | です |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| eiga | desu | nichiyoubi | no | eiga | wa | tanoshii | yasumi | desu |
| *movie* | *is* | *Sunday* | *'s / of (links nouns)* | *movie* | *topic marker* | *fun* | *day off* | *is* |

**3. Yuki:** そうですか。私(わたし)のしゅみはりょうりです。  
*Soudesuka. Watashi no shumi wa ryouri desu.*  
Oh, really? My hobby is cooking.  
Literal: `oh-really. I 'S hobby TOPIC cooking is`

**5. Yuki:** ええ。これは私(わたし)のケーキです。つめたいケーキです。どうぞ。

## Teacher script

Welcome back! Today's pattern puts adjectives in front of nouns. For a **い** adjective, you just put it straight in front of the noun. For a **な** adjective, you add **な** in between. Either way it means: a hot coffee, a quiet shop.  
> 🔊 おいしい コーヒー  
Delicious coffee. The adjective **おいしい** goes first, then the noun **コーヒー**. Nothing in between.  
> 🔊 しずかな まち  
A quiet town. Here the adjective is **しずか**, quiet, and it needs the little linker **な** before the noun **まち**, town.  
So there are two slots. First, the describing word. Second, the noun. Good news: the order is the same as English. Describer first, thing second.  
Let's build one. Start with the noun.  
> 🔊 コーヒー  
Coffee. Now put the **い** adjective **あつい**, hot, in front.  
> 🔊 あつい コーヒー  
Hot coffee. Add **です** at the end.  
> 🔊 あつい コーヒーです。  
It's hot coffee. And put a topic in front with **は**.  
> 🔊 これは あつい コーヒーです。  
This is hot coffee.  
Now a **な** adjective. Start with the noun **みせ**, shop.  
> 🔊 みせ  
Shop. Now **しずか**, quiet, plus the linker **な**.  
> 🔊 しずかな みせ  
A quiet shop. Now the topic and **です**.  
> 🔊 あれは しずかな みせです。  
That over there is a quiet shop.  
Now a swap. Same skeleton, new words. Ken says this about his Sunday movies:  
> 🔊 たのしい やすみです。  
It's a fun day off. Swap in **つめたい**, cold, and **ケーキ**, cake:  
> 🔊 つめたい ケーキです。  
It's a chilled cake. Same shape, different pieces.  
Here's the common mistake. Don't add **な** to a **い** adjective. Hot coffee is **あつい コーヒー**, never with **な**. Memory trick: **い** words can stand on their own; **な** words need a little helper to hold the noun's hand.  
And notice: in front of **です**, quiet was just **しずかです**. The **な** only shows up when a noun follows.  
This snaps right onto what you know. An adjective plus noun is just a bigger noun, so it fits anywhere a noun goes: after **は**, before **です**, even with **の**. Like this:  
> 🔊 どようびの よていは しずかな みせの コーヒーです。  
Our plan for Saturday is coffee at a quiet café. Now let's hear Yuki and Ken use it.
